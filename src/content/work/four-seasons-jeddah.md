I lead BIM implementation for the interior fit-out package on this landmark hotel and residences project.

## What I do

- Resolve multidisciplinary clashes in Revizto and Navisworks before drawings are released to site engineers.
- Build BIM models from CAD drawings and manage them through the design and construction stages.
- Author the AIM, PIM, LOIN, BEP and Revit templates for the project.
- Audit subcontractor models against ISO 19650 and run model quality checks.
- Submit weekly models through ACC, Aconex and Revizto and present weekly BIM progress.
